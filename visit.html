Visiting FAQ

Where is Trio Restaurant &amp; Lounge?

3817 Hull Street Rd, Richmond, VA 23224, on the Southside.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is available at the restaurant.

What are the hours?

Tuesday 11 AM–9 PM, Wednesday and Thursday 12–9 PM, Friday and Saturday 12 PM–1 AM, Sunday 12–5 PM. Closed Monday.

How do I make a reservation?

Call (804) 298-3775. For tables and sections on event nights you can also DM @triorestaurantnloungerva on Instagram.

Can I bring kids?

Yes, Trio is kid-friendly until 6 PM and has a kids menu. Evenings are for grown folks, and some events are 21+ or 30+.

Do you deliver?

Yes, through DoorDash. Online pickup orders go through Trio’s Toast page.
