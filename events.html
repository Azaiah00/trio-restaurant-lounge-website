Event night FAQ

What’s happening at Trio this week?

Tuesday lunch specials and happy hour, Wings & Whiskey Wednesdays, R&B Thursdays, Friday Sessions with DJs, Ladies Night Out with KISS 105.7 on Saturday, and Game Day day parties on Sundays during football season.

Is Ladies Night free?

Yes. Ladies Night Out with KISS 105.7 runs Saturday 9 PM–1 AM, and ladies get in free all night. It’s a 30+ event.

Are events 21 and over?

Some events are 21+ and some, like Saturday Ladies Night, are 30+. Trio is kid-friendly until 6 PM.

Is there a cover charge on event nights?

Some event nights have a cover or a seating deposit. Check Trio’s Instagram or call ahead for that night.

How do I reserve a table or section for an event?

Call (804) 298-3775 or DM @triorestaurantnloungerva on Instagram for tables and sections. Bottle service is available.

Where can I find the latest lineup?

Lineups and guest DJs change, so follow @triorestaurantnloungerva on Instagram for this week’s flyers.
