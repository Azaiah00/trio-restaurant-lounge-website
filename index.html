Questions, answered

Do I need a reservation at Trio?

Reservations are taken by phone at (804) 298-3775. They are the best way to lock in a table on busy event nights, and tables and sections can also be requested by Instagram DM.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk in any time during regular hours. On Friday and Saturday event nights, calling ahead is the safest way to get a seat.

What are Trio’s hours?

Tuesday 11 AM–9 PM, Wednesday and Thursday 12–9 PM, Friday and Saturday 12 PM–1 AM, and Sunday 12–5 PM. Trio is closed on Mondays.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is available at the restaurant at 3817 Hull Street Rd.

Is Trio kid-friendly?

Yes, until 6 PM, with a kids menu of tenders, wingettes and shrimp from $10.99. After that it’s a grown-folks lounge, and some events are 21+ or 30+.

Is there a cover charge?

Some event nights have a cover or a seating deposit. Call or check Trio’s Instagram for the night you’re planning.

How much does a meal cost?

Baskets and sandwiches with fries run $11.99–$22, entrées and seafood dinners $17.99–$29.99, and Trio platters $31.99–$34.99. Sides are $5.25 and signature cocktails $8–$17.

When is happy hour?

Tuesday 3–7 PM, Wednesday and Thursday 4–7 PM, and Friday 4–8 PM, with food and drink specials.

Can I get delivery or takeout?

Yes. Order delivery on DoorDash, or order pickup online through Trio’s Toast page.

Can I reserve a section or bottle service?

Yes. Tables and sections can be reserved by calling (804) 298-3775 or sending a DM to @triorestaurantnloungerva on Instagram. Bottle service is available, with Patrón bottles at $250.
